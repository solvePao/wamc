WAMC — Android phone camera for Mac

1. Drag WAMC.app to Applications and open that copy. If opened from this
   disk image, use Install & Relaunch in the setup panel instead.
2. WAMC automatically requests activation of its camera extension. In
   System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions,
   enable WAMC > Android Camera. Approve any macOS authentication prompt.
3. Use Enable Recommended Defaults to turn on automatic connection,
   camera startup, and launch at login. Allow camera and Local Network
   access when asked. Existing preferences are otherwise preserved.
4. Open WAMC on Android and allow its camera. For USB, use a data cable,
   enable USB debugging on Android, and approve this Mac on the phone.
   USB needs Android Platform Tools (adb); the setup panel checks for it.
   For Wi-Fi, use the same network and choose Trust & Connect for the phone.
5. Select Android Camera in FaceTime > Video or Photo Booth > Camera.
   The OBS logo means OBS Virtual Camera is selected. OBS can coexist.

Open Setup & Diagnostics from the WAMC menu-bar menu any time. It checks
installation, signing, extension approval, and virtual-camera registration.
“Sending phone video” is a host delivery check. Confirm moving video in
your meeting app as the final test. A host preview alone is insufficient.

macOS requires user approval of privacy permissions and camera extensions.
WAMC cannot silently change these switches or select a camera in other apps.

This is a locally signed Apple Development build for Macs registered in
its provisioning profile, not a notarized public-distribution release.
The profile and signing certificate expire; rebuild with renewed signing
assets when needed. Never disable SIP, Gatekeeper, or camera privacy to
work around a signing error.
